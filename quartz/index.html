SolarEX Quartz SiO₂

Passive SiO₂ surface engineering for solar glass.

SolarEX Quartz is a transparent SiO₂ nano-film engineered to reduce adhesion of dust, pollen, mineral particulates, water and grime on photovoltaic glass — without requiring UV activation.

Surface engineering

The passive pathway for soiling control.

SolarEX Quartz is engineered for operating environments where passive surface repellence is the technically appropriate solution. It requires no UV activation, no photocatalytic trigger and no on-site energy input to function.

Quartz reduces cleaning burden by lowering contaminant adhesion and improving easy-clean surface behavior from point of cure.

01

No UV activation required

Quartz delivers passive surface repellence without relying on irradiance, making it suitable for diffuse-light and high-latitude environments.

02

Hydrophobic + oleophobic behavior

The SiO₂ nano-film imparts water-repellent and oil-repellent properties that reduce adhesion of water, oily particulates and grime.

03

Reduced cleaning burden

Lower adhesion energy supports longer cleaning intervals and faster cleaning cycles where site conditions fit the Quartz pathway.

Mechanism

How Quartz works.

Quartz operates through SiO₂-based surface chemistry applied as a transparent 100–150 nm nano-film to photovoltaic glass. The film modifies surface energy so water forms high contact angles and rolls away instead of spreading and drying into mineral-deposit films.

Surface mechanism

The SiO₂ film reduces adhesion energy at the glass-contaminant interface. Dust, pollen, mineral particulates and grime are less able to bond to the treated surface compared with untreated glass.

Mechanism boundary

Quartz is an easy-clean pathway, not an active photocatalytic pathway. Its mechanism is physical surface-energy modification: inorganic, stable and UV-independent.

Best fit

Where Quartz performs best.

Quartz is the no-UV pathway. It is preferred where UV-dependent photocatalytic activation cannot be relied upon, or where inorganic/mineral particulate soiling dominates the operating profile.

Dust, pollen and mineral soiling

Optimized for inorganic and mineral-particulate soiling profiles including airborne dust, pollen and calcium/mineral accumulation.

Low-UV / high-latitude sites

Useful where photocatalytic activation is less consistent, including Nordic, northern European, Scottish and overcast installations.

Rainfall-assisted cleaning

Reduced adhesion energy allows rain events to carry away loosely held particulates more effectively than untreated glass.

Water-scarce sites

Supports extended cleaning intervals and lower wash frequency where site conditions and cleaning strategy align.

Application engineering

Application and surface preparation.

Correct application engineering is critical to Quartz performance. Surface preparation directly determines adhesion quality and long-term coating performance.

1

Clean

Remove dust, grime, soiling and residues, including surfactants and tensides.

2

Apply

Use HVLP spray or pre-impregnated wipe/lint-free cloth at appropriate surface temperature.

3

Cure

Pre-cure around 30 seconds; ready cure ~5–30 minutes; full cure approximately 24 hours.

4

Verify

Confirm uniform coverage and readiness; rain exposure tolerance is indicative around one hour post-application.

Pathway selection

When Quartz is the correct choice.

Quartz is preferred where passive surface repellence and UV-independent performance match the site profile. Titan is preferred where organic or biological contamination dominates and sufficient UV exposure is available.

Choose Quartz when

Dust, pollen, mineral deposits, low-UV conditions, rainfall-assisted cleaning or water-constrained cleaning economics dominate the site profile.

Review Titan when

Organic, biological or industrial contamination dominates and sufficient UV exists to support active photocatalytic behavior.

Technical FAQ

Quartz — common technical questions.

Does Quartz need UV light to function?

No. Quartz is a passive SiO₂ surface-engineering pathway and does not require UV irradiance to activate or maintain its surface properties.

How does Quartz improve maintenance cleaning?

Quartz lowers soiling adhesion, supporting longer intervals and faster cleaning cycles where the site profile fits the passive pathway.

Does Quartz affect panel light transmission?

No measurable optical transmittance penalty is stated in the SolarEX source material for the 100–150 nm transparent film.

Is Quartz better than Titan for all sites?

No. Quartz is preferred for passive UV-independent repellence. Titan is preferred for UV-supported organic/biological contaminant breakdown.
