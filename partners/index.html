Partner principle

Partnership should scale the right message, not generic coating claims.

SolarEX partner development is built around route control: the correct surface pathway, the correct buyer type, the correct evidence class and the correct next action. Regional partners should help customers understand operating fit before procurement or pilot scale-up.

Partners must keep SolarEX Quartz SiO₂ and SolarEX Titan TiO₂ distinct, avoid unsupported performance promises, and use technical review where site conditions or proof requirements are not yet clear.

Quartz SiO₂

Passive easy-clean route

For UV-independent hydrophobic/oleophobic PV glass protection and lower contaminant adhesion in relevant operating contexts.

Titan TiO₂

Active UV-supported route

For UV-dependent photocatalytic and hydrophilic/superhydrophilic review where organic or atmospheric contamination is relevant.

Partner FAQ

Commercial partner questions.

Can partners use their own performance claims?

No. Public partner messaging must follow approved SolarEX claim-control language and source-grounded evidence boundaries.

Is regional exclusivity offered on this page?

No. This page opens partner inquiry and qualification. Any commercial rights require separate review and agreement.

Which product should partners lead with?

Start with site fit. Quartz is the passive SiO₂ route; Titan is the active UV-dependent TiO₂ route. Technical review should handle uncertain cases.

Is there a live partner form?

No. Use info@solarex.no for partner inquiries until backend partner-form submission is approved and validated.
