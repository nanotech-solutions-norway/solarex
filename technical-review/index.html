Review principle

Technical review prevents wrong-pathway specification.

SolarEX review is designed to prevent a site from being forced into the wrong product route. Quartz and Titan solve different surface-engineering problems and must be evaluated against the operating environment.

When the contamination profile, UV availability, cleaning model or evidence need is unclear, the correct next step is technical review rather than product assumption.

Quartz

Passive SiO₂ route

Use for UV-independent hydrophobic/oleophobic easy-clean behavior where dust, salt, pollen, sand, grime and bird lime are primary.

Titan

Active TiO₂ route

Use where UV-supported photocatalytic and hydrophilic/superhydrophilic behavior is technically relevant.

Review

Unclear or mixed sites

Route to technical review when contamination, UV, cleaning model or evidence requirements are not yet defined.

Review routes

One review can lead to several controlled next steps.

The review outcome should be a practical route, not a generic sales answer.

Product route

Quartz or Titan selection

Confirm the correct coating pathway based on mechanism and site conditions.

Evidence route

Documentation review

Identify which source material, application instructions or study context is relevant.

Commercial route

ROI and pilot planning

Define assumptions, monitoring logic and pilot boundaries before commercial scale.

Review workflow

From site context to controlled recommendation.

The review process keeps technical, evidence and commercial questions connected.

1

Submit context

2

3

Match evidence

Link the case to documentation, application instructions, study material or ROI inputs.

4

Define next step

Recommend product route, additional data request, pilot protocol or commercial discussion.

Required inputs

Better inputs produce better recommendations.

Technical review is most useful when the site context is concrete.

Site and asset

Country, region, PV asset type, approximate glass area and operational context.

Soiling profile

Operations

Cleaning interval, water access, current cleaning cost, O&M constraints and desired decision date.

Review FAQ

Technical review questions.

Can I submit a review without choosing Quartz or Titan?

Yes. That is the preferred route when the technical fit is not yet clear.

Will review replace a controlled pilot?

No. Review can define whether a pilot is needed and what it should measure.

Can ROI be assessed from review data?

Yes, if the inputs are sufficient. ROI remains assumption-based until site-specific data is validated.
