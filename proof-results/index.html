Evidence principle

Proof must stay connected to mechanism and conditions.

SolarEX results are interpreted through coating mechanism, application quality, climate, contamination type, cleaning strategy and monitoring context.

This prevents the most common coating-website error: turning a valid result from one condition into an unsupported universal claim.

01

Mechanism traceability

Quartz evidence must remain tied to passive SiO₂ repellence. Titan evidence must remain tied to active UV-supported TiO₂ photocatalysis.

02

Condition traceability

Every result is interpreted with its site, test or scenario assumptions: climate, UV, water exposure, soiling type and baseline.

03

Commercial relevance

Evidence is used to support pilot design, ROI modeling and route selection before larger commercial specification.

Titan evidence

Active TiO₂ proof context.

Titan evidence is evaluated through the UV-dependent photocatalytic mechanism and the contamination profile that makes active TiO₂ technically relevant.

PV³ Experts study context

The Titan PV³ Experts source is treated as study evidence for solar-module TiO₂ evaluation under documented conditions. It supports technical review and pilot planning where UV and contamination conditions align.

Mechanism-dependent interpretation

Titan must not be interpreted as a passive, no-UV route. Its evidence is meaningful where UV exposure supports photocatalytic activity and hydrophilic/superhydrophilic behavior.

Claim boundary

Evidence supports decisions. It does not replace site review.

SolarEX results should be used to decide whether a route is technically and commercially worth reviewing for a specific site. They should not be copied into unqualified performance guarantees.

Approved use

Technical review, pilot selection, route comparison, ROI modeling and commercial discussion based on documented assumptions.

Blocked use

Universal performance claims, unsupported warranty statements, invented certification language or route-blending between Quartz and Titan.

FAQ

Proof and results — common questions.

Are SolarEX results universal guarantees?

No. Results are interpreted according to mechanism, assumptions and operating conditions before specification.

Can Quartz and Titan evidence be merged?

No. Quartz is passive SiO₂. Titan is active UV-dependent TiO₂. Their evidence must stay separated by mechanism.

How should ROI evidence be used?

ROI models should be treated as structured scenarios and adjusted to project-specific energy price, cleaning cost, irradiance and soiling assumptions.

What is the next evidence step?

Run a technical review and, if appropriate, a controlled pilot with baseline and monitoring criteria.
