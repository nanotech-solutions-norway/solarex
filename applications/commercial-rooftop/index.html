Rooftop route principle

Rooftop applications are operational as much as technical.

Commercial rooftops often combine urban dust, pollen, grime, bird lime, access restrictions, safety rules and variable cleaning economics. The correct SolarEX route depends on both the contamination profile and the maintenance model.

Quartz SiO₂ is the passive UV-independent easy-clean route. Titan TiO₂ is the active UV-dependent route only where contamination and UV conditions support photocatalytic and hydrophilic/superhydrophilic review.

Pathway selection

Rooftop route selection should follow the contamination profile.

Quartz SiO₂ can be reviewed where passive easy-clean behaviour is the objective. Titan TiO₂ should be considered only where active UV-dependent photocatalytic or hydrophilic/superhydrophilic behaviour is relevant to the surface challenge.
