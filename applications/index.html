Application principle

Application fit depends on surface conditions.

PV glass exposure varies by region, asset type and cleaning model. SolarEX routing should review contamination before product selection.

Quartz SiO₂ is the passive UV-independent route. Titan TiO₂ is the active UV-dependent photocatalytic and hydrophilic/superhydrophilic route.

Environment

What reaches the glass?

Review dust, salt, pollen, grime, mineral residue, bird lime, organic film or mixed contamination.

Mechanism

Which route fits?

Separate Quartz SiO₂ passive easy-clean logic from Titan TiO₂ active UV-supported logic.

Operations

What must improve?

Review cleaning interval, water access, O&M cost pressure, output concern and pilot objective.

Route selection

Choose the surface pathway by mechanism fit.

The first application decision is whether the site needs passive UV-independent easy-clean behavior, active UV-supported photocatalysis/hydrophilicity, or deeper review.

SolarEX Quartz SiO₂

Passive UV-independent hydrophobic/oleophobic route for easy-clean PV glass protection and reduced contaminant adhesion in relevant contexts.

Review Quartz

SolarEX Titan TiO₂

Active UV-dependent photocatalytic and hydrophilic/superhydrophilic route where organic or atmospheric contamination is technically relevant.

Review Titan

Operating factors

Capture the conditions that change the route.

Soiling profile

Dust, sand, salt, pollen, grime, mineral deposits, bird lime, organic film or mixed contamination.

UV and climate

High UV, low UV, rainfall, humidity, coastal exposure, desert exposure and seasonal effects.

Cleaning model

Current interval, water access, mechanical cleaning, manual cleaning, cleaning cost and downtime sensitivity.

Asset profile

Rooftop, utility-scale, pilot, region, module type, glass area, access and safety constraints.

Controlled next step

Route the application before specifying the product.

Start with technical review when the application has mixed contamination, uncertain Quartz/Titan fit, ROI questions or pilot requirements.
