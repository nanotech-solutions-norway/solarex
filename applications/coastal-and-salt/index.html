Coastal and Salt PV Glass Applications

Coastal PV sites should be reviewed for salt aerosol, humidity, mixed grime, rainfall, cleaning access and glass-surface residue before specifying SolarEX Quartz SiO₂, SolarEX Titan TiO₂ or a controlled technical review route.

Coastal route principle

Salt exposure should be reviewed with site context.

Coastal applications are not only a salt question. Review should consider marine aerosol, humidity, rainfall, dust mix, local grime, cleaning method and whether residue creates a persistent surface-management issue.

Quartz SiO₂ is the passive UV-independent easy-clean route. Titan TiO₂ is an active UV-dependent route only where the site conditions support photocatalytic and hydrophilic/superhydrophilic review.

Exposure

Salt and humidity

Review marine aerosol, humidity, rainfall, dry periods and salt-residue behaviour on glass.

Mix

Salt plus grime

Coastal sites may combine salt, dust, pollen, industrial grime and organic contamination.

Route

Mechanism-led selection

Keep Quartz SiO₂ and Titan TiO₂ separated before product recommendation.

Review factors

Information needed for coastal/salt routing.

Salt profile

Marine aerosol, salt deposition, frequency of exposure, rainfall wash-off and dry residue behaviour.

Humidity and rainfall

Humidity cycle, rain pattern, wet/dry phases and whether residue persists between cleaning events.

Mixed contamination

Dust, pollen, grime, bird lime, atmospheric particles or organic film combined with salt exposure.

Cleaning model

Water access, equipment access, manual/mechanical cleaning, safety and operational cost sensitivity.

Pathway selection

Coastal cases require Quartz/Titan separation.

Quartz SiO₂ is the passive easy-clean route for reduced contaminant adhesion in relevant conditions. Titan TiO₂ should be reviewed only where active UV-dependent photocatalytic or hydrophilic/superhydrophilic behaviour fits the contamination profile.

Pilot framing

Control assumptions before scale-up.

Coastal/salt applications should define the baseline cleaning model, salt exposure, mixed contamination profile and treated/control comparison before a larger commercial route is selected.

Before pilot

Record salt exposure, rainfall pattern, cleaning interval, treated area, control area and target outcome.

During pilot

Track surface condition, cleaning effort, residue behaviour and whether the route supports the intended operational objective.

Next controlled step

Review the coastal/salt case before specifying the route.
