Contact principle

Every inquiry should enter the correct route.

SolarEX contact handling should separate technical route selection from commercial discussion. This avoids premature product claims, keeps Quartz SiO₂ and Titan TiO₂ boundaries clear, and makes each inquiry easier to qualify.

The correct first step depends on whether the inquiry is about site conditions, pilot design, documentation, procurement, partnership or general information.
