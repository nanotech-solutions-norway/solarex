Case-study principle

Case studies must show context, not isolated claims.

SolarEX case-study content is structured to show the relevant mechanism, evidence class, location or test context, operating assumptions and the decision it supports.

This keeps SolarEX commercially useful while preserving claim discipline: Quartz and Titan evidence must not be mixed, generalized or turned into universal promises.

01

Mechanism first

Quartz case contexts explain passive SiO₂ easy-clean behavior. Titan contexts explain active UV-dependent TiO₂ behavior.

02

Evidence class second

Each case is identified as monitored study, ROI scenario, field observation, controlled comparison or pilot hypothesis.

03

Decision third

The case must support a defined next decision: technical review, pilot design, documentation review, ROI modeling or specification support.

Titan study context

Active TiO₂ cases need UV and contamination fit.

Titan case-study content is strongest when the site has sufficient UV exposure and organic, biological or industrial-atmospheric contamination that makes photocatalytic surface behavior relevant.

What the Titan case can support

Technical review, pilot planning, photocatalytic mechanism explanation and buyer education around active TiO₂ behavior in a solar module context.

What the Titan case must not imply

It must not imply Titan is UV-independent, universally suitable, or directly comparable to passive Quartz SiO₂ without mechanism separation.

Quartz regional context

Passive SiO₂ cases support cleaning-burden and ROI review.

Quartz case-study content is strongest when the operating problem is dust, pollen, salt, mineral deposits, bird lime, grime or surface adhesion that affects cleaning burden and recovery of glass condition.

Europe / Nordic context

Passive easy-clean behavior can be evaluated for lower-UV, rainfall-assisted, maintenance-sensitive and high-latitude PV operations.

Middle East / GCC context

Passive easy-clean behavior can support pilot discussion for dust pressure, water constraints and high-cleaning-burden sites.

Case-study FAQ

Common case-study questions.

Can a case study prove all future results?

No. A case context supports technical and commercial evaluation under similar conditions, but it does not replace site-specific review.

Can Titan study results be used for Quartz?

No. Titan is active TiO₂. Quartz is passive SiO₂. Evidence must remain separated by mechanism.

Can ROI scenarios be published as guarantees?

No. ROI scenarios are assumption-based models and must be adjusted for project-specific energy price, cleaning cost and site behavior.

What makes a future SolarEX case publishable?

A clear baseline, product route, application method, monitoring period, results interpretation and approved claim boundary.
