Project principle

A SolarEX project starts with mechanism fit.

PV glass coating selection should not start with a generic anti-soiling claim. It should start with the operating problem: dust, pollen, mineral deposits, organic film, biofouling risk, UV availability, water access, cleaning method and target outcome.

The project process keeps SolarEX Quartz and SolarEX Titan separated so each pathway is evaluated against the conditions where it can be technically and commercially relevant.

01

Mechanism before marketing

Quartz and Titan are selected by mechanism, not by one-size-fits-all coating language.

02

Site conditions before assumptions

Climate, UV, water exposure, soiling type, cleaning interval and access constraints shape the project path.

03

Evidence before scale

Evidence supports pilot design and decision-making before full rollout or regional partner activity.

Project routes

Choose the correct route before specifying a product.

SolarEX projects are organized into three route types: technical review, controlled pilot and commercial specification support.

Technical review

Route selection and site fit

Used when the site needs a structured decision between Quartz, Titan, documentation, ROI modeling or pilot planning.

Controlled pilot

Treated vs control evaluation

Used when the asset owner or O&M team needs defined baseline, treated area, monitoring period and project-specific success criteria.

Specification support

Commercial project package

Used when SolarEX is moving toward procurement, partner deployment, documentation handoff or larger-scale rollout.

Pathway selection

Project routing by contamination profile.

Correct pathway selection protects technical accuracy and commercial trust.

Site signalLikely routeProject interpretation
Dust, pollen, mineral deposits, low UVQuartz SiO₂Passive, UV-independent hydrophobic/oleophobic easy-clean route for reduced contaminant adhesion and maintenance burden.
Organic film, biofouling, atmospheric residues, sufficient UVTitan TiO₂Active UV-dependent photocatalytic route with hydrophilic/superhydrophilic behavior after activation.
Mixed or unclear contaminationTechnical reviewHold product selection until site conditions and contamination profile are clarified.
Commercial scaling or partner deploymentSpecification supportUse controlled documentation, application instructions, claim boundaries and evidence packages before rollout.

Pilot design

Controlled pilots need a clean baseline.

A SolarEX pilot should be designed so the result can support a decision. That means defining the treated area, control area, cleaning behavior, monitoring period, weather context, contamination observations and commercial assumptions before application.

Minimum pilot inputs

Location, asset type, approximate module area, current cleaning interval, water access, soiling type, UV context, application method and expected decision date.

Minimum pilot outputs

Route recommendation, coating pathway, application plan, monitoring plan, ROI assumptions, risk notes and decision criteria for continuation or scale-up.

Evidence integration

Evidence is used to shape the project, not replace it.

Quartz scenarios and Titan study material help define reasonable project assumptions. They do not remove the need to evaluate local conditions before specification.

Quartz

Passive SiO₂ evidence

Used for cleaning-burden, easy-clean, ROI scenario and low-UV project discussions where passive repellence is the relevant mechanism.

Titan

Active TiO₂ study context

Used for UV-supported photocatalytic and hydrophilic/superhydrophilic evaluation where organic or biological contamination is relevant.

Review

Project-specific assumptions

Energy price, cleaning cost, monitoring setup, weather and O&M strategy must be reviewed before commercial conclusions are drawn.

Project FAQ

Common project planning questions.

Can a project start without choosing Quartz or Titan?

Yes. Technical review is intended for situations where the correct pathway is not yet clear.

Does SolarEX require a pilot before specification?

Project-specific validation is recommended where risk, scale or operating conditions require controlled decision support.

Can Quartz and Titan be mixed in one project?

They can be reviewed in the same project, but their mechanisms and evidence must remain separate.

What information is needed first?

Location, asset type, surface area, soiling profile, cleaning method, UV context and the decision the project must support.
