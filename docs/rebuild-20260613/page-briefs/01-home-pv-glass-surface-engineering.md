## Approved content notes

- SolarEX is a PV glass surface-engineering platform, not a generic solar panel coating.
- Quartz is the passive SiO₂ route for UV-independent hydrophobic/oleophobic easy-clean behavior.
- Titan is the active TiO₂ route for UV-supported photocatalytic and hydrophilic surface behavior.
- Correct pathway selection depends on UV availability, contamination profile, geography, cleaning burden and asset-owner objectives.
- Titan evidence must stay tied to study context.
- Quartz ROI scenario values must stay tied to stated assumptions.
- Avoid universal claims such as eliminating cleaning or guaranteed payback.
