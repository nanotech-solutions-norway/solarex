Which pathway fits your site?

Which pathway fits your site?

Quartz is the UV-independent SiO₂ route for dust, salt, pollen, sand, grime and bird lime where easy-clean recovery matters. Titan is the UV-dependent TiO₂ route for organic, biological and atmospheric contamination where photocatalytic behavior is technically suitable.
