Why surface engineering matters

Target the adhesion mechanism, not only the cleaning symptom.

PV glass surfaces accumulate dust, pollen, bird lime, salt, industrial pollutants and biological residues over their operational life. Contaminant adhesion affects optical interface quality and can contribute to measurable output variability under sustained soiling conditions.

SolarEX is structured around mechanism-led pathway selection so asset owners, EPCs and O&M teams can evaluate the correct coating route before scale application.

Optical interface protection

Cleaner glass surfaces support more stable PV operation by reducing contaminant bonding to the glass substrate.

Reduced contaminant adhesion

Lower bonding affinity for dust, pollen, bird lime, salt, grime and environmental residues.

Lower cleaning burden

Supports faster, less abrasive and less water-intensive maintenance cycles where site conditions allow.

Mechanism-led selection

Quartz and Titan are selected through UV availability, contamination profile, geography and O&M objectives.

Two pathways. Different mechanisms.

SolarEX is not a single generic coating.

It is a surface-engineering platform with two distinct product pathways, each targeting a different physical and chemical mechanism.

Passive pathway

SolarEX Quartz — SiO₂

Ultra-thin SiO₂ nano-scale layer for hydrophobic and oleophobic easy-clean behavior. No UV activation required.

  • Anti-stick behavior for dust, bird lime, pollen, salt, grime and industrial pollution.
  • Best fit for inorganic/mineral soiling, low-UV environments and high-latitude sites.
  • Technical basis includes 100–150 nm layer, 9H pencil hardness and documented application data.
Review Quartz SiO₂

Active pathway

SolarEX Titan — TiO₂

Alcohol-based TiO₂ nanoparticle coating for UV/natural-sunlight activated photocatalysis and hydrophilic film behavior.

  • Supports anti-fog, easy-rinse and self-cleaning behavior under sufficient UV exposure.
  • Best fit for organic, biological and atmospheric contamination with sufficient UV availability.
  • UV availability is functionally important and must be evaluated before selection.

Technical questions — short answers

Decision-support answers for EPC, O&M and procurement teams.

What is the difference between Quartz and Titan?

Quartz is a passive SiO₂ coating with hydrophobic and oleophobic easy-clean behavior. Titan is an active TiO₂ coating with UV/sunlight-activated photocatalysis and hydrophilic sheeting behavior.

Which pathway is right for low-UV or high-latitude sites?

Quartz is the preferred pathway because its passive mechanism operates independently of UV availability. Titan requires site-specific assessment where UV availability is limited.

Does SolarEX eliminate cleaning requirements?

No. SolarEX coatings support reduced cleaning burden, faster cleaning and less abrasive maintenance cycles, but they do not eliminate scheduled maintenance or replace site-specific O&M programs.

How should performance and ROI values be interpreted?

Values should be interpreted by evidence class. Titan values are study-specific, Quartz ROI values are scenario-based, and site parameters are used to refine expected project value.
