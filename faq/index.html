FAQ principle

Questions must preserve pathway accuracy.

SolarEX FAQ answers are written to prevent the two most common specification errors: treating Quartz and Titan as interchangeable, and converting contextual evidence into universal claims.

For commercial planning, the correct answer usually depends on contamination profile, UV availability, cleaning model, water access, asset type and the decision the customer needs to make.

Quick answers

Most common SolarEX questions.

These short answers are intended for fast route selection. Detailed review should still use the Technology, Quartz, Titan, Proof / Results and Documentation pages.

What is the difference between Quartz SiO₂ and Titan TiO₂?

SolarEX Quartz is the passive SiO₂ route for UV-independent easy-clean PV glass protection. SolarEX Titan is the active TiO₂ route for UV-supported photocatalytic and hydrophilic/superhydrophilic behavior.

Can Quartz and Titan be used interchangeably?

No. The two pathways use different mechanisms and should be selected according to site conditions, contamination profile and UV context.

Which product is best for low-UV or shaded conditions?

Quartz is the preferred pathway when UV availability is limited and passive hydrophobic/oleophobic easy-clean behavior is the primary technical need.

Which product is best for organic or biofilm-prone contamination?

Titan may be relevant when sufficient UV is available and the contamination profile makes photocatalytic activity technically useful.

Quartz SiO₂ FAQ

Passive PV glass protection questions.

Does Quartz require UV?

No. Quartz is a passive SiO₂ pathway with UV-independent hydrophobic/oleophobic easy-clean properties when properly applied.

What does Quartz help with?

Quartz supports reduced adhesion of dust, pollen, sand, grime, bird lime and mineral residue so cleaning can become easier and surface condition can recover more efficiently.

Does Quartz replace cleaning?

No. Quartz supports easier cleaning and reduced adhesion, but cleaning intervals should be adapted to the site, not removed by default.

Where does Quartz perform best?

Quartz is relevant for dust, mineral, pollen, salt, grime and low-UV contexts where passive easy-clean behavior is the correct route.

Titan TiO₂ FAQ

Active photocatalytic pathway questions.

Does Titan work without UV?

Titan requires UV activation for its photocatalytic mechanism. It should not be specified as a UV-independent pathway.

What does Titan help with?

Titan is relevant where UV-supported photocatalytic activity and hydrophilic/superhydrophilic behavior can support organic, biological or atmospheric contamination management.

Is Titan the same as Quartz?

No. Titan is active TiO₂ and Quartz is passive SiO₂. Their mechanisms, evidence context and route selection are different.

Where does Titan perform best?

Titan should be reviewed for UV-rich environments where organic film, biological contamination or industrial-atmospheric residues are relevant.

Evidence FAQ

Proof, ROI and case-study questions.

Are SolarEX results universal guarantees?

No. Evidence is interpreted according to pathway, assumptions, site conditions and test context before specification.

Can ROI scenarios be published as guaranteed payback?

No. ROI models are assumption-based scenarios and must be adjusted to project-specific electricity price, cleaning cost, irradiance, soiling and application cost.

How should study evidence be used?

Study evidence should support technical review and pilot design while staying connected to the study method, operating conditions and product mechanism.

What should be done before scale-up?

Use technical review to define route selection, baseline, treated/control logic, monitoring period, application protocol and decision criteria.

Application FAQ

Surface preparation and application questions.

Why is surface preparation important?

Application quality depends on clean, suitable PV glass conditions. Surface contamination, residues and poor preparation can undermine coating performance.

Should application be performed without documentation?

No. Application should follow controlled instructions, surface-preparation requirements and project documentation.

Can SolarEX be specified from FAQ answers alone?

No. The FAQ supports orientation. Final route selection should be handled through technical review when project conditions matter.
