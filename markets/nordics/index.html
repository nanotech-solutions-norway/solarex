Nordics route principle

Nordic conditions require careful mechanism fit.

Nordic market discussions should account for low-UV seasons, rain and snow, salt exposure, coastal humidity, rooftop access, cleaning safety and seasonal production value.

Quartz SiO₂ offers the passive UV-independent easy-clean route. Titan TiO₂ should be discussed only where active UV-dependent behaviour is relevant and realistic for the operating context.
